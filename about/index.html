About Carolina De La Cerda

Carolina De La Cerda is a strategic HR leader with 11+ years of experience serving as a trusted advisor to senior leaders across manufacturing, operations, engineering, and quality organizations. She currently works at Cepheid as an HR Business Partner, supporting over 500 employees across day and swing shifts.

Her career started as a Social Worker, which gave her a strong people-centered foundation and the ability to understand different perspectives, build trust, and navigate complex situations with empathy. She later earned her MBA from the University of Michigan Ross School of Business, strengthening her ability to connect business strategy with people strategy.

What differentiates Carolina is her ability to combine empathy with business discipline. She cares deeply about people but also focuses on creating structure, improving processes, driving accountability, and delivering business results. She uses a coaching approach — asking thoughtful questions before offering solutions, helping leaders think differently about people and business decisions.

Based in Lodi, California, Carolina has worked across small and large organizations as well as the public sector in Chile and the United States, partnering with a wide variety of leaders, teams, and employee populations.
