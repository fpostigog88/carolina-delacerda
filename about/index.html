About Carolina De La Cerda

Carolina De La Cerda is a scientist specializing in molecular diagnostics with extensive experience in assay development and infectious disease detection. She currently works at Cepheid, a leading molecular diagnostics company, where she develops and validates assays for the detection of infectious diseases using PCR and qPCR technologies.

Her work spans the full assay development lifecycle — from experimental design and primer/probe design through verification, validation, and technology transfer to manufacturing. She combines deep technical expertise in molecular biology with a passion for creating diagnostic tools that directly impact patient care.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, Carolina holds a Ph.D. in Molecular Biology / Biotechnology and has research experience from top institutions in Chile and the United States.
